## Visual Architecture & Design Principles
- **Aesthetic**: Dark editorial men's fashion & grooming campaign style.
- **Palette**: Near-black (`#0A0A0A`), Charcoal (`#141414`), Warm Cream (`#F3EFE7`), Muted Gold (`#D4B77D`).
- **Typography**: Oswald (Display Headlines), Plus Jakarta Sans (Clean Body), Cormorant Garamond (Editorial Accents).
- **Icons**: 100% Inline Lucide SVGs (Zero icon font dependencies).

## Page Structure
1. **Sticky Header** — Typographic logo lockup, desktop links, Call & Directions buttons, mobile menu toggle.
2. **Full-Screen Editorial Hero** — High contrast photo, `LOOK SHARP. FEEL SHARP.` headline, Call & Directions CTAs.
3. **Quick Info Bar** — 4-column brand, location, phone & postcode bar.
4. **Gentlemen's Menu** — Structured service categories without fabricated prices.
5. **The Cut Is In The Detail** — Editorial split feature on precision, detail & style.
6. **Gallery ("The Work Speaks")** — Portfolio grid showcasing cuts & shop imagery.
7. **About The Shop ("More Than A Haircut")** — Brand story with verified storefront photo.
8. **Before You Visit** — Location, phone, booking & hours information cards.
9. **Client Reviews Placeholder** — Dev-ready section for verified Google reviews.
10. **Follow The Corner** — Social proof section with Facebook integration.
11. **Find Your Way To The Corner** — Location card & embedded Google Maps iframe.
12. **Final CTA** — High-impact conversion section before footer.
13. **Footer** — Complete footer with legal copyright, links & address.
14. **Mobile Sticky Bar** — Fixed bottom call & directions bar on mobile devices.
